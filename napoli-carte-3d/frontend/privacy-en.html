This policy covers Scopa, Scopone, Briscola, Sette e mezzo, Asso piglia tutto, Rubamazzetto and Chiodo 121, together with the app’s online services.

1. Information used by the app

Account and sign-in

If you register, Google Firebase Authentication handles the email, password and name you provide. Google sign-in uses the authentication information and email authorised through Google. The app uses a Firebase user identifier to recognise your account, verify or recover access and attribute rewards. The authentication service may process IP addresses and technical information for operation and security. Guest play is also available; rewarded videos require sign-in.

Profile, photos and games

Your nickname, avatar, optional photo, preferences, some statistics and virtual balance are stored on your device. During online play the server processes session identifiers, name, room, moves, cards and scores. In the classic games it may receive your cropped photo and visibility choices: “Solo avatar” does not send the photo. Other modes show it to participants, friends in the match or a private room according to your choice. In-game friendships relate to the match; the game profile is not a cloud profile synchronised across devices.

Chat and audio

Messages are sent to the server and shown to other people in the room. If you enable audio, the app requests microphone permission and transmits your voice to participants. Classic games may use a server audio relay; Chiodo uses WebRTC connections. Connections also process IP addresses and network parameters, with connection services such as Google STUN. The game service does not maintain a voice-recording archive. Spoken game announcements use your selected Android text-to-speech engine, which may have its own network features.

Advertising and consent

The app integrates Google AdMob and Google’s UMP consent platform. Advertising services may process IP addresses, approximate location derived from IP, advertising or app identifiers, ad interactions and diagnostics for delivery, measurement and abuse prevention. Advertising choices and any providers are listed in the consent form. Personalisation depends on your preferences and applicable requirements.

Rewards and support

For rewarded videos, your Firebase identifier is sent to AdMob and the server to attribute the reward. The reward ledger stores a transaction identifier, Firebase identifier, amount of 350 virtual credits and receipt date to validate rewards and prevent duplicates. If you contact support, we process your email address and the information you provide to respond and handle the request.

2. Purposes and legal grounds

We use information needed to provide the features you request: accounts, online games, chat, audio and rewards. Service security and fraud or abuse prevention serve our legitimate interests. Advertising processing that requires consent depends on your choice; legal obligations provide a separate ground where applicable. Providing a photo and enabling the microphone are optional. Without information needed for a feature, that feature may be unavailable.

3. Who may receive information

  • Other players: your name, match information and content you share in a room, subject to the features and visibility settings you select.
  • Google/Firebase: authentication; Google AdMob/UMP and providers listed in the form: advertising, consent and reward validation.
  • Render: hosting for game services, supported audio transport and reward infrastructure. Services may generate technical logs needed for their operation.
  • Support and competent authorities: to handle your requests or comply with applicable obligations.

Providers may process data outside the European Economic Area. Their documents describe processing locations and transfer safeguards, including contractual clauses or adequacy decisions where applicable: Firebase, Google and Render. You may contact us for information about the app’s processing.

4. Retention

  • Account: authentication information remains with Firebase while the account is active and until its deletion is handled.
  • Local data: your profile, photos, preferences and wallet remain on the device until removed. You can clear app data through Android settings or uninstall the app. Automatic Android backup is disabled for the app.
  • Matches: rooms currently reside in server memory. Classic rooms expire after 4 hours of inactivity when playing, 30 minutes when finished, or 45 minutes when waiting; a waiting room also expires 2 hours after creation. Chiodo matches expire after 30 minutes of inactivity and rooms without a match after 60 seconds. Match update requests count as activity.
  • Chat, shared photos and voice: chat retains up to the latest 80 messages per room. Messages and profiles may remain while other participants continue using the room and are removed with the room. Relayed audio uses temporary transmission buffers and is not archived as a recording.
  • Reward ledger: there is no automatic expiry. The ledger recognises previously awarded rewards and is included in a deletion request. Any retention needed for legal obligations or abuse prevention is assessed and explained, including its reason and duration.
  • Support and technical data: requests are retained for handling and the necessary record of the response; providers apply their service conditions to their technical logs and data. Retention depends on the purpose and applicable obligations.

5. Your choices

You can change your profile and visibility in the app and revoke microphone permission through Android settings. Review advertising consent through Negozio → Guarda un annuncio → Preferenze privacy when UMP makes the form available for your region and circumstances. Starting a rewarded video is voluntary. Android settings also provide advertising identifier controls.

See Google’s explanation of how it uses information from apps using its services. Declining personalisation does not necessarily remove all technical processing needed for permitted ads.

6. Account and data deletion

You can request deletion of your account and associated data without reinstalling the app. The dedicated page explains what is covered and how to send a request to support. Updated app versions also link to it from Account and the Negozio store.

Request account and data deletion

Support handles requests after verifying identity. Opening the page or an email app does not automatically delete data. In versions up to 6.6, “Elimina account” removed Firebase sign-in access; you can use the same support channel for associated data and the reward ledger.

7. Rights and contact

Where applicable, you may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability or object to processing. You can withdraw consent without affecting prior processing. Email alo986761986@gmail.com; we normally respond within one month and explain any justified extension. You may complain to the Italian data protection authority or the authority for your place of residence.

8. Security, minors and changes

HTTP communications with the servers use HTTPS; the audio relay uses secure WebSockets. Access to rewards requires authentication. Microphone permission is requested for audio. No system removes every risk: only share profile and chat information you intend other participants to see.

If you act for a minor and believe action is needed concerning their account or data, contact us. Changes to processing or services will be reflected here with an updated date and further information where required.
