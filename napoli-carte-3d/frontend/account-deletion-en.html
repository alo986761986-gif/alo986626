Request without reinstalling the app

Contact GAMES STUDIOS support

You can request deletion of your NAPOLI CARTE 3D account and associated data even if you uninstalled the app or can no longer sign in.

Prepare deletion request email

Recipient: alo986761986@gmail.com
Subject: Account and data deletion request - NAPOLI CARTE 3D

The button opens your email app with a draft. Review the fields and send it to submit the request. If no email app opens, write directly to the address above. This page does not send a request automatically.

How to identify your request

  1. Where possible, write from the email used for your account and say that you want to delete the account and associated data.
  2. You may include your player name and information that helps identify the profile. Reinstalling the app or knowing a technical identifier is not required.
  3. If you cannot use the account email, explain this in your message. We ask only for information needed to verify that the request concerns your data.

Only essential identifying information is needed: passwords, sign-in codes and payment information are not required.

What the request covers

  • The app’s Firebase sign-in account: email, name and account identifier.
  • The reward ledger linked to the Firebase identifier: transactions, awarded credits and dates.
  • Any game-service data still available and attributable to the identified profile.

You may also request deletion of specific information without deleting the account; explain this in your request. Deleting your NAPOLI CARTE 3D account does not delete your personal Google account.

How requests are handled

Support handles deletion after verifying identity. Opening this page or an email draft does not perform deletion automatically. We normally respond within one month and explain any necessary clarification or justified extension.

Our response confirms the outcome. If information must be retained for legal obligations or justified abuse prevention, we explain the information, reason and retention period. The reward ledger currently has no automatic expiry: removal of account-linked information is handled through this request.

Information on your phone and in matches

Your profile, photos, preferences and balance may remain on your phone. Remove them through Android Settings → Apps → NAPOLI CARTE 3D → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Clearing phone data alone does not delete the account or server reward ledger.

Temporary room data expires according to the criteria in the privacy policy. Shared messages may remain in the room while other players continue using it.

If you already used “Elimina account” in a version up to 6.6, you can still contact us about associated data: that control removed Firebase sign-in access without requesting complete deletion of server data.

Access from the app

Updated versions offer Eliminazione account e dati in the Account screen and the Negozio store. It opens these same instructions, also available without sign-in.
